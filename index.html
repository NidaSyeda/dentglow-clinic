What services are available at DentGlow?+

The clinic lists dental treatments including cleaning, fillings, root canal, braces and aligners, implants, whitening, gum care, extractions, crowns, dentures and digital X-rays. Skin, laser and hair services are listed separately above.

What are the clinic hours?+

The clinic’s existing website lists Monday to Sunday, 10:00 am to 10:00 pm. Call ahead to confirm holiday hours and current availability.

How do I book a visit?+

Call +91 89561 55703 or send a WhatsApp enquiry. The clinic team will confirm available timings; a message does not itself confirm an appointment.

Where is DentGlow located?+

Beed Bypass Road, beside Arfat Masjid and near Rasheed Mamu Complex, Hamid Colony, MIDC, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra 431001. Use the Google Maps panel above for navigation.
